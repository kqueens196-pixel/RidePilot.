# Pricing Rules

LAUNCH_PERIOD_DAYS = 90
LAUNCH_MONTHLY_PRICE = ₹99
STANDARD_MONTHLY_PRICE = ₹150
ONE_DAY_PASS_PRICE = ₹10

During days 1-90:
- Eligible new user can receive first eligible ride/order free.
- Monthly subscription can be purchased for ₹99.

From day 91:
- Monthly subscription is ₹150/month.
- The launch free-first-ride offer ends.
- Existing subscriptions renew at ₹150/month after the introductory period.

One-Day Pass:
- ₹10
- Show exact expiry timestamp before purchase.
- Never silently convert it to monthly billing.
